# powerbi-blinkit-sales-dashboard

Power BI Dashboard analyzing BlinkIt (quick-commerce) sales and delivery performance.

## Key Insights
- Identified the highest-performing product categories based on sales and quantity sold.
- Tracked overall business KPIs — Total Sales, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Average Rating, and Return Rate %.
- Analysed order trends across years, months, and days to spot seasonal and weekly patterns.
- Compared payment method usage (UPI, Card, Cash, Wallet, etc.) to understand customer preferences.
- Visualised city-wise sales distribution across India using map visuals.
- Monitored order status (Delivered, Cancelled, Returned, etc.) to evaluate fulfilment efficiency.
- Measured customer satisfaction through rating distribution analysis.
- Tracked daily and monthly sales/quantity trends to identify peak demand periods.

## Project Highlights
- End-to-end Power BI Dashboard Development.
- Interactive Business Intelligence Reporting with dynamic slicers (Category, City, Payment Method, Order Status, Year, Month).
- DAX Measures & Calculated Columns (Total Sales, Total Orders, Average Order Value, Average Rating, Return Rate %).
- Data Cleaning & Transformation with Power Query.
- Star-schema Data Model — Fact table (blinkit_sales_data) linked to Dim_Customer, Dim_Product, Dim_DarkStore, and a Calendar date table.
- Geographic Sales Analysis across Indian cities.
- Customer Rating & Payment Behaviour Analytics.

## Business Value
This dashboard enables stakeholders to:
- Monitor quick-commerce business performance in real time.
- Identify top-performing product categories and cities/dark stores.
- Evaluate customer payment preferences and satisfaction levels.
- Improve delivery efficiency by tracking order status and return rates.
- Spot seasonal and day-wise demand patterns to plan inventory and staffing.
- Support data-driven strategic decision-making.
